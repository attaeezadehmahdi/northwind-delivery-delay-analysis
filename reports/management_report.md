# Management Report: Analysis of Factors Affecting Order Shipment Time

## Introduction

This report examines various factors that may influence order shipment time. The objective is to analyze the shipment process from different perspectives and identify potential areas for improvement.

The analysis considers several dimensions, including destination country, time periods (year, quarter, and month), shipping company, product, product category, and order quantity.

Five hypotheses were developed and evaluated using the available data. The findings are presented below.

## 1. Impact of Shipping Country on Order Shipment Time

The analysis indicates that the average shipment time for orders destined for **Ireland is approximately 37% higher than the overall average**. Similarly, shipment time for orders destined for **Sweden is approximately 25% higher than the overall average**.

In contrast, this pattern was not observed in other European countries. For example, orders destined for **Finland were shipped more than 30% faster than the overall average**.

## 2. Impact of Time Period on Order Shipment Time

Based on the analysis, time does not appear to have a significant impact on average order shipment time.

In other words, the average time between order placement and shipment remained approximately **8 days** across the years, months, and quarters examined.

## 3. Impact of Product Type on Order Shipment Time

The analysis identified six products with average shipment times more than **25% above the overall average**. Among these products, two had shipment times exceeding the overall average by more than **90%**.

However, orders associated with these six products accounted for approximately **8.3% of total orders**. Given their relatively small share of overall order volume, their impact on the overall shipment process was limited.

## 4. Impact of Shipping Company on Order Shipment Time

The analysis did not reveal any significant differences in average shipment time among the shipping companies.

Therefore, based on the available data, the shipping company does not appear to have a significant impact on order shipment time.

## 5. Impact of Order Quantity on Order Shipment Time

In this analysis, order quantities were divided into eight groups to investigate whether larger orders require more time to ship.

The results showed that orders containing between **39 and 49 items** had a longer average shipment time, approximately **25% above the overall average**.

However, this pattern was not observed among groups with higher order quantities.

Further examination of the standard deviation indicated that heterogeneous observations within this group contributed to the increase in average shipment time.

Consequently, the available evidence suggests that the number of items per order does not have a consistent or significant relationship with shipment time.

---

## Conclusion

Based on the analysis, the destination country and certain products show noticeable differences in average shipment time compared with the overall average.

In contrast, no clear pattern of influence was identified for time periods, shipping companies, or order quantities.

These findings provide a basis for further investigation into the shipment process and may help identify opportunities for operational improvement.
